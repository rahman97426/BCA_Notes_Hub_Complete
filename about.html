About BCA Notes Hub

A free educational resource built for BCA students studying at institutions affiliated with Aryabhatta Knowledge University (AKU), Patna.

What is BCA Notes Hub?

BCA Notes Hub is a student-built, student-focused platform that collects, organises and presents study materials for the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) programme. It is designed to make exam preparation simpler, more organised and more accessible for all BCA students.

This is an independent educational resource and is not the official website of Aryabhatta Knowledge University (AKU) or any affiliated college.

Our Purpose

  • Provide well-organised, semester-wise study notes for BCA subjects
  • Offer previous year question papers (PYQs) to help students understand exam patterns
  • Share official syllabus documents so students always know what to study
  • Provide quick-revision cheatsheets for important topics
  • Support effective exam preparation with targeted resources

Resources Available

📖 HTML Notes

Interactive, browser-readable notes covering all BCA Semester II subjects including C Programming, OS & UNIX, System Analysis & Design and more.

🔥 Previous Year Questions (PYQs)

Past examination question papers for AKU Patna BCA subjects to help students understand patterns and focus on important topics.

📕 Official Syllabus

Subject-wise syllabus documents for BCA Semester II covering all 5 subjects: Business English, Numerical Techniques, SAD, C Programming and OS & UNIX.

📝 Cheatsheets

Quick-reference cheatsheets for all 5 Semester II subjects for rapid last-minute revision before exams.
